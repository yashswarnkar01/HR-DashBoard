# HR-DashBoard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition, department-wise turnover, job satisfaction, age groups, overtime, and salary distribution to support data-driven HR decisions.

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive HR Data Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes employee attrition, workforce distribution, job satisfaction, department-wise turnover, age groups, overtime, and salary bands.

The dashboard helps understand employee turnover patterns and provides insights that can support better human resource management.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Employees:** 1,500
- **Attrition Count:** 226
- **Attrition Rate:** 15.07%
- **Average Monthly Income:** 69,385

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Attrition Count by Job Satisfaction
Analyzes employee attrition across job satisfaction levels from 1 to 4.

### 2. Attrition Count by Department
Shows employee attrition across departments such as Sales, Research and Development, Operations, Information Technology, Finance, and Human Resources.

### 3. Attrition Count by Job Role
Displays employee attrition across different job roles to identify areas that may require attention.

### 4. Attrition by Age Group
Analyzes how employee attrition varies across different age groups.

### 5. Attrition by Overtime
Compares employee attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not.

### 6. Employee Count by Salary Band
Shows employee distribution across Medium, Low, Upper Medium, and High salary bands.

## Interactive Features
- Department slicer to filter the dashboard.
- Interactive charts for exploring employee attrition patterns.
- Multiple visualizations for workforce analysis.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard creation and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and KPI calculations.
- **Data Modeling:** Organizing data for HR analysis.

## Key Insights
- The overall employee attrition rate is 15.07%.
- The dashboard records 226 employee attritions out of 1,500 employees.
- Attrition varies across departments and job roles.
- The dashboard compares attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not.
- Employee distribution differs across salary bands.
- Job satisfaction and age-group analysis help explore possible patterns in employee turnover.

## Business Value
This dashboard helps HR teams monitor employee attrition, identify workforce patterns, and explore factors associated with employee turnover. These insights can support employee retention strategies and better workforce planning.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform HR data into meaningful visual insights. It combines key performance indicators, interactive filters, and visual analysis to support data-driven HR decisions.
